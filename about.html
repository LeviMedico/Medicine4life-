Medicine4life is written by Sushil Dethaliya, a medical student who spends as much time reading medical device validation studies as standard clinical textbooks. This site exists because most mainstream coverage of medical technology falls into one of two camps: breathless, non-technical press releases, or extremely dense journal publications written exclusively for researchers.

The goal here is the space in between — plain-language, rigorous explanations of what a new tool, wearable, or clinical discovery actually does, what the evidence supports, and what it doesn't, written by someone training to make these decisions on the front lines.

New posts cover AI-assisted diagnostics, continuous biomarkers, precision genomics, and other modern technologies reshaping day-to-day medicine, with an emphasis on what's ready for real-world clinic deployment versus what's still promising but unproven.
