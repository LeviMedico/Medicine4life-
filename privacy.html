Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Medicine4life ("we," "us," or "this site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit medicine4life.org.

Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to read this site. We collect limited information in the following ways:

Usage data. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and general location and device information (such as browser type and country). This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Cookies. This site uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — for analytics and advertising purposes, described in more detail below. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended if you do.

Information you provide directly. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive whatever information you choose to share in that message (such as your email address or phone number). We use this only to respond to you and do not sell or share it with third parties.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors engage with the site. Google Analytics collects information anonymously and reports website trends without identifying individual visitors. You can learn more about how Google collects and processes data at Google's Privacy & Terms page, and you can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Children's privacy

Medicine4life is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. Since we collect very little identifying information in the first place, most requests can be resolved simply by contacting us directly at sushil@medicine4life.org.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out at sushil@medicine4life.org.
