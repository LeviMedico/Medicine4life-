---
name: Sickle Cell Disease
overview: A genetic blood disorder where red blood cells form a rigid, sickle
  shape instead of a flexible disc, causing blockages in blood flow, chronic
  pain episodes, and organ damage over time.
whyItMatters: It's one of the first diseases successfully treated with
  CRISPR-based gene editing therapy, marking a major proof-of-concept for gene
  editing as a curative approach rather than lifelong symptom management.
treatment: Pain management, blood transfusions, and hydroxyurea remain standard;
  CRISPR-based gene therapy is now approved in some countries for eligible
  patients, though access and cost remain significant barriers.
