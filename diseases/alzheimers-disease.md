---
name: Alzheimer’s Disease
overview: A progressive neurological disorder that damages brain cells and
  gradually affects memory, thinking, and daily functioning. It is the most
  common cause of dementia worldwide.
whyItMatters: The disease develops years before symptoms appear. Growing
  research focuses on detecting early changes and targeting abnormal proteins
  like amyloid and tau.
treatment: Current treatments mainly manage symptoms, while newer therapies aim
  to slow progression by targeting disease pathways. Research areas include
  anti-amyloid antibodies, biomarkers, and precision medicine.
