---
name: Type 2 Diabetes
overview: A chronic condition where the body becomes resistant to insulin or doesn't produce enough of it, leading to elevated blood sugar levels over time.
whyItMatters: Poorly managed blood sugar increases risk for heart disease, kidney damage, nerve damage, and vision loss over time.
treatment: Lifestyle changes, oral medications like metformin, and — increasingly — GLP-1 and dual-agonist injectable drugs, which improve blood sugar control and often cause significant weight loss as well.
---
