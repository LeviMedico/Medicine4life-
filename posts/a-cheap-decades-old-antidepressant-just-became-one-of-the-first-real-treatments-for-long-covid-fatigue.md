***Long COVID affects an estimated 65 million people worldwide, and for most of them, the defining symptom isn't respiratory — it's fatigue so persistent and disabling that it reshapes daily life. Until now, medical guidance for that fatigue has mostly amounted to pacing and symptom management, because no medication had strong trial evidence behind it. A new randomized trial just changed that, and the drug behind it is one you've probably heard of for a completely different reason.***

## The trial

The REVIVE-TOGETHER trial enrolled 399 adults in Brazil, all with significant fatigue lasting at least 90 days after a confirmed COVID-19 infection. Participants were randomly assigned to one of three groups for 60 days: fluvoxamine, an inexpensive antidepressant already widely used and sold as Luvox; metformin, a common diabetes drug; or a placebo.

Both drugs had a real scientific rationale for being tested — but neither had actually been tried for this specific use in a rigorous trial before.

## What the results actually showed

Fluvoxamine worked. Metformin didn't.

<p class="pull">Two drugs went in with a plausible rationale. Only one came out with real evidence behind it.</p>

By day 60, people taking fluvoxamine showed a significant reduction in fatigue compared to placebo, and that improvement held up through day 90. Patients on fluvoxamine were also nearly 50% more likely than the placebo group to report their fatigue had dropped to a mild level within just 30 days. Quality-of-life scores improved too, and side effects were actually less frequent with fluvoxamine than with either metformin or placebo.

Metformin, despite earlier evidence that it can reduce the \*risk\* of developing Long COVID if taken during the initial infection, showed no benefit once fatigue was already established — the metformin arm of the trial was stopped early for futility.

## Why this result matters beyond the numbers

This is being described as one of the first pieces of genuinely strong trial evidence for \*any\* Long COVID treatment — not a repurposed hope, not a small pilot study, but a proper randomized, placebo-controlled trial with a clear result. Fluvoxamine is also cheap and already well understood by prescribers, which matters enormously for how quickly a finding like this could actually reach patients compared to a brand-new, expensive drug still working through approval.

## The honest limits

The trial only followed patients for 90 days, so it doesn't yet answer whether the benefit holds up over months or years. It also measured fatigue specifically — Long COVID often comes with a cluster of other symptoms, like brain fog and post-exertional malaise, that this trial wasn't designed to evaluate. Researchers are clear that Long COVID is a complex, multi-system condition, and one drug improving one symptom isn't the same as a cure.

Still, for a condition that has offered patients very little beyond "manage it as best you can," a low-cost pill with a 99% statistical probability of outperforming placebo on fatigue is a genuinely meaningful data point — not a final answer, but the clearest evidence yet that this particular symptom is treatable.
