---
name: Multiple Myeloma
overview: A cancer of plasma cells — a type of white blood cell made in the bone
  marrow that normally helps fight infection.
whyItMatters: Newer immune-based therapies, including CAR-T cell therapy and
  bispecific antibodies, have meaningfully improved outcomes for patients whose
  disease has stopped responding to standard treatment.
treatment: Combination drug therapy, stem cell transplant for eligible patients,
  and immune-based therapies for relapsed or treatment-resistant cases.
