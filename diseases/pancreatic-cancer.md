---
name: Pancreatic Cancer
overview: A cancer that begins in the pancreas, an organ behind the stomach that helps with digestion and blood sugar regulation. It's often diagnosed at an advanced stage because early symptoms are vague or absent.
whyItMatters: Most cases are caught after the cancer has spread. Many pancreatic tumors also carry RAS gene mutations, which have historically been difficult to target with drugs, though that is starting to change.
treatment: Surgery when caught early, plus chemotherapy; targeted therapies like RAS inhibitors are an active, fast-moving area of research and clinical trials.
