Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Medicine4Life ("we," "us," or "Medicine4Life") handles information when you use the Medicine4Life Android learning app or visit medicine4life.org.

Medicine4Life Android app

No account is currently required. The app does not currently require a user profile to access its learning features.

Learning progress. Quiz results, flashcard reviews, MedQuest performance, streaks, mastery, saved AI flashcards, and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device. The app provides controls to reset learning progress and clear saved AI flashcards.

AI Clinical Tutor. When you submit an AI Tutor question, the text of your request and limited recent conversation context are transmitted securely to our Supabase-hosted function and processed by Google Gemini to generate an educational response. Do not enter names, medical-record numbers, contact details, identifiable patient information, or other sensitive personal data into the tutor.

Content requests. The app connects to Medicine4Life services hosted through Supabase to retrieve published articles, game content, and medicine information. These requests may include standard technical information needed to provide and secure the service, such as network and request metadata.

Study reminders. Reminder preferences are saved on your device. Android may ask for notification permission so the app can deliver reminders you choose to enable. Medicine4Life does not use this permission to read your notifications.

Medicine4Life website

Usage data. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site—for example, pages viewed, approximate region, browser type, and device category.

Cookies. The website may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Information you provide directly. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond to you and manage the communication.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors engage with the site and to report aggregate website trends. Google may process device, browser, approximate location, and interaction information according to its own policies. You can learn more at Google's Privacy & Terms page, and you can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate Medicine4Life, including Supabase for backend infrastructure, Google Gemini for AI-generated educational responses, Google Analytics for website analytics, Google AdSense for website advertising, and Cloudflare for website delivery. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

How we use and share information

We use information only to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Medicine4Life; provide requested learning functionality; respond to communications; and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with the service providers described above when needed to deliver their services, or where required by law.

Data controls

You can reset locally stored learning progress and clear saved AI flashcards from the app's preferences. You can disable study reminders through the app or Android settings. For information you sent directly to us, you may request access or deletion by contacting us.

Children's privacy

Medicine4Life is designed for medical learners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, object to processing, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you. Contact us at sushil@medicine4life.org to make a request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out at sushil@medicine4life.org.
