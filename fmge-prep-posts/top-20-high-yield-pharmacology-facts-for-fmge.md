* Top 20 High-Yield Pharmacology Facts for FMGE

  1. Acetaminophen (Paracetamol) overdose is treated with N-acetylcysteine (NAC).

  2. Heparin acts immediately and is monitored using aPTT, while warfarin is monitored using PT/INR.

  3. ACE inhibitors (e.g., enalapril) commonly cause dry cough and angioedema due to increased bradykinin.

  4. Aminoglycosides (gentamicin, amikacin) cause nephrotoxicity and ototoxicity and are contraindicated in myasthenia gravis.

  5. Macrolides (azithromycin, erythromycin) can cause QT interval prolongation.

  6. Fluoroquinolones (ciprofloxacin, levofloxacin) are associated with tendon rupture and should be avoided in pregnancy and young children.

  7. Tetracyclines cause teeth discoloration and inhibit bone growth; avoid during pregnancy and in children under 8 years.

  8. Metronidazole causes a disulfiram-like reaction with alcohol.

  9. Rifampicin is a potent CYP450 enzyme inducer and turns body fluids orange-red.

  10. Isoniazid (INH) can cause peripheral neuropathy, prevented with vitamin B6 (pyridoxine).

  11. Atropine is the antidote for organophosphate poisoning, while pralidoxime (2-PAM) regenerates acetylcholinesterase.

  12. Naloxone rapidly reverses opioid overdose, whereas flumazenil reverses benzodiazepine overdose.

  13. Insulin drives potassium into cells, making it useful in the emergency treatment of hyperkalemia.

  14. Loop diuretics (furosemide) cause hypokalemia, whereas spironolactone causes hyperkalemia.

  15. Digoxin toxicity presents with yellow-green vision (xanthopsia), nausea, and arrhythmias. The antidote is digoxin immune Fab.

  16. β-blockers are contraindicated in acute severe asthma because non-selective agents can cause bronchospasm.

  17. Calcium channel blockers



  **Verapamil/Diltiazem**: affect the heart.

  **Amlodipine/Nifedipine**: mainly affect blood vessels.


  18. Statins inhibit HMG-CoA reductase and can cause myopathy and elevated liver enzymes.


  19. Aspirin irreversibly inhibits COX-1 and COX-2, reducing thromboxane A₂ and platelet aggregation. Its antiplatelet effect lasts for the life of the platelet (7–10 days).


  20. Methotrexate inhibits dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR). Leucovorin (folinic acid) is used as rescue therapy to reduce toxicity.
